# MEMBER'S ELITE — WEBSITE DESIGN STANDARD V2

## NON-NEGOTIABLE #1 — HOMEPAGE ANIMATION
The homepage animation is a VISUAL-ONLY CANVAS.

NEVER overlay on the animation:
- headlines
- body copy
- CTAs/buttons
- badges
- labels
- counters
- captions
- promotional copy
- HTML/CSS marketing text

The site header may remain structurally above the page, but no marketing copy is positioned inside/on top of the animation canvas. Conversion content begins AFTER the animation ends.

## CREATIVE DIRECTION
Member's Elite must not look like a predictable dark sports-agency pitch deck. It should feel like the world's coolest private athletic club: fresh turf, premium basketball hardwood, daylight, white architecture, black structure, brushed silver, selective gold, real sport texture, technology and performance data.

### Surface ratio
- 55–65% bright sport environment: green turf, hardwood, daylight facility, white/off-white space.
- 15–20% clean digital UI: athlete metrics, booking, program and dashboard interfaces.
- 10–15% black/gold brand structure: navigation, buttons, labels, trims, premium cards.
- 5–10% dark cinematic Member's Elite graphics: used as deliberate feature interruptions, never as the entire page language.

## RHYTHM
Animation → bright turf/hardwood conversion → choose sport → training → performance/data → cinematic dark interruption → management → bright membership club → camps/events → Athlete HQ → legacy/footer.

## ONE DOMINANT IDEA PER VIEWPORT
Every major viewport gets one visual priority, one primary headline and one primary action. Do not stack diagrams, six cards, multiple banners and dense copy in the same visual frame.

## TURF RULES
Use real-looking fresh-cut green turf, field markings, training cones, sleds, hurdles, timing gates and premium facility architecture. Turf is a major brand surface, not a generic football background. Avoid muddy fields, cheap school-gym aesthetics, neon gaming green, fake stadium crowds and excessive gold glow.

## HARDWOOD RULES
Basketball sections use premium clean hardwood with strong court lines, natural arena/gym daylight and black/gold Member's Elite equipment. Do not make every basketball scene a dark NBA tunnel. The court itself should provide warmth and visual breathing room.

## DARK GRAPHIC RULES
Existing Member's Elite black/gold cinematic graphics are approved storytelling assets. Use them for full-bleed feature moments, management/legacy transitions, recruiting/NIL and brand storytelling. Never tile them as cards. Never put several dark graphics side by side. Give each one breathing room.

## COLOR
Primary environment: turf green / hardwood maple / warm white.
Brand anchors: black, metallic gold, silver.
Backgrounds should not default to #050505. Use white, bone, pale gray, turf and hardwood surfaces throughout the experience. Gold is an accent, not a flood fill.

## TYPOGRAPHY
Large athletic editorial type. Short headlines. High contrast. No tiny deck-style paragraphs. Body width should remain readable. Use uppercase selectively for athletic impact, not every sentence.

## CARDS
Maximum 4 primary cards visible in a standard desktop row. Prefer image-led cards. Cards must have meaningful hierarchy and generous spacing. No endless gold-outlined black boxes. On mobile, cards become swipeable or stacked with one clear action.

## NAVIGATION
Primary: Train, Basketball, Football, Programs, Camps, Management, Athlete HQ, About. Persistent BOOK NOW CTA. Mobile navigation must be thumb-friendly and never obscure the animation.

## CONVERSION RULES
Training is now a primary front door alongside management. Every training page must reach a bookable program within 1–2 interactions. Basketball routes to Coach Tae programs; Football routes to Coach Justin programs. Prices must come from the canonical Member's Elite catalog rather than conflicting hard-coded copies where integration is available.

## MOBILE
Mobile is designed independently, not shrunk desktop. Hero animation remains text-free. Full-bleed sport visuals. Sticky Book Now is allowed only outside the animation canvas. Minimum tap targets 44px. No horizontal overflow. No tiny multi-column diagrams.

## MOTION
Use restrained premium motion: field-line reveals, scoreboard number transitions, subtle parallax, card lift, court/turf wipe transitions. Never animate everything. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Animation cannot impair booking or navigation.

## ASSET QA
Use approved Member's Elite assets only. Do not redraw the logo. Do not alter real athlete faces. Never substitute unrelated athlete/property imagery for an approved asset. Every asset must have a defined page/section assignment before production.

## RELEASE BLOCKERS
Do not ship if:
- any marketing text overlays the homepage animation;
- the site returns to predominantly black sections;
- basketball and football are visually indistinguishable;
- pricing conflicts with canonical catalog;
- booking CTA dead-ends;
- mobile has clipped/overflowing content;
- dark cinematic graphics are tiled/repeated;
- placeholder athlete claims, fake metrics or unverified accomplishments are presented as fact.
